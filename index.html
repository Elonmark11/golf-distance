You place the reticle in screen pixels, so a 4 K stream shown at 1× wastes most of its resolution — roughly 3.6 source pixels hide behind every screen pixel. Zooming is what converts that resolution into accuracy: at 150 yd it takes the error from about ±16 yd at 1× to ±2 yd at 8×. Always zoom in before taking a reading.

Measurement

Not standardized — the USGA only recommends at least 7 ft, and courses use 7–8 ft. Distance scales directly with this, so an 8 ft stick assumed to be 7 ft reads 14% short.

How to calibrate

1. Measure a tall object of known height (a door works) and enter it as the flagstick height.
2. Stand a tape-measured 10 m away, bracket it, tap Calibrate, enter 10 m.
3. Move to 30 m and check the reading without recalibrating. It must read true.

Off by a constant ratio → focal length is wrong. Off by a constant offset → reticle placement is wrong. Don't trust it on a course until step 3 passes.

Rules

USGA Rule 4.3a(1) permits distance-measuring devices unless a Local Rule forbids them. Slope-adjusted readings are not allowed — this app deliberately has no slope feature, so it stays conforming.
